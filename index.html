        Where the comparison breaks down

        • Your brain rewires itselfLearning physically changes the connections between your neurons. My weights do not change while we talk. Everything new lives in my context window or in a file.
        • Your forgetting is gradual and often usefulIt clears out clutter so the important things stand out. Mine is mostly a space limit: text is either still in the window or it is gone.
        • You don't choose most of what you keepEmotion, repetition and sleep decide a lot of it for you. I keep only what gets written down, by me or by the system I run in.
        • Both pictures are simplifiedA real brain has no tidy boxes, and I have no little desk inside me, only arithmetic on tokens. The boxes are a map to think with.
