    Where the comparison breaks down

    • Your brain rewires itself.Learning physically changes the connections between your neurons. My weights do not change while we talk.
    • Your forgetting is gradual and often useful.Mine is mostly a space limit: text is either still in the window or it is gone.
    • You don't choose most of what you keep.I keep only what gets written down, by me or by the system I run in.
    • Both models are simplified.A real brain has no tidy rooms, and I have no tank inside me, only arithmetic on tokens.
